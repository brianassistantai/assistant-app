Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What it accesses. Only the Google permissions the account owner approves on Google's consent screen, such as their own Google Tasks.

How data is used. Only to carry out the owner's own requests (for example, adding or checking off a task). Nothing else.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared, or sent to anyone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Storage. The access key is stored only on the owner's own computer, readable by that one user.

Removing access. Any time at myaccount.google.com, Security, Third-party connections. Access stops at once.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
